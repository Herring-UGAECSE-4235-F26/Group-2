Overview

PARMCO is a project designed to allow users to control a stepper motor, with a Raspberry Pi 4, via a phone app. The app acts as the interface, allowing users to control various aspects of the motor's behavior while being user friendly and safe to use. Such controls include speed adjustment, spin direction control, zero calibration, angle set, position feedback, and more. PARMCO aims to provide students with an educative and informative experience regarding the use of AI by using AI as the primary author of the project. The website serves as a platform to showcase the design process, progress, experiences, and results of the project.

PARMCO is created over a seven week period with three progress checkpoints and one final checkpoint. Checkpoints are designed to ensure steady progress and provide measurements of success and goal achievements. Additionally, each checkpoint is designed to guide teams towards the final objective while allowing for creativity and problem solving. Various design constraints and requirements must be met, in order to remain compliant with the project specifications and guidelines. Project requirements are discussed in more detail under the Design targets section. Checkpoints goes into detail about the design process and results for each checkpoint, leading up to the final project delivery.
